# Sources for the expanded question bank

GeoVoice's 40 added questions cover maps and navigation (41–50), climate and weather (51–60), natural landmarks (61–70), and countries and cultures (71–80). Each new category has three easy, four medium, and three hard questions. Explanations in `backend/questions.js` are paraphrased; source pages are not copied into the app.

| Question IDs | Primary references |
|---|---|
| 41–50 | [USGS: Finding Your Way With Map and Compass](https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/finding-your-way-map-and-compass), [USGS: What Do Maps Show?](https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/what-do-maps-show), [USGS: Topographic Map Symbol Guide](https://www.usgs.gov/ngp-standards-and-specifications/us-topo-map-symbol-guide) |
| 51–60 | [NOAA: climate and weather](https://gml.noaa.gov/outreach/info_activities/pdfs/PSA_comparing_climates.pdf), [NOAA: weather station measurements](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/crn/elements.html), [NOAA: monsoons](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/k-12-education/severe-weather/what-monsoon), [NOAA: seasons](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/our-environment/solar-phenomena/changing-of-the-seasons) |
| 61–70 | [NPS: Grand Canyon geology](https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/nature/grca-geology.htm), [USGS: oxbow lakes](https://eros.usgs.gov/earthshots/oxbow-lakes), [USGS: caves](https://www.usgs.gov/geology-and-ecology-of-national-parks/geology-mammoth-cave-national-park), [USGS: sea arches](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/holei-arch), [USGS: plate tectonics](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/99/pdf/gip99.pdf) |

Images and alt text are mapped by stable question ID in `dist/visuals.js`. The new SVG illustrations are original GeoVoice artwork with no answer text or country labels. Existing generic photos are reused for physical features; they are not claimed to show the named landmark.
