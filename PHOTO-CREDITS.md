## Question-specific context

Assignments in dist/visuals.js use question IDs, never answer indices. Capital questions display the flag of the country already named in the prompt, not a city landmark. Mountain, desert, river, cave, lake, and waterfall prompts use generic corresponding scenes. Unlabeled original globe artwork supports questions where a map, highlighted line, or isolated country silhouette could reveal the answer. Landform definitions use a close shoreline scene rather than an outline that demonstrates the answer. Captions describe the subject, not an answer location. The photographs illustrate environments and are not claimed to depict the named site.
